### g20 (store) — My photos look blurry, help.

> Blurry photos usually come down to one (or more) of these causes:

1. **Camera shake** – shutter speed too slow for handheld shooting (or focal length). Rule of thumb: use a shutter speed at least 1/(focal length in mm), and faster for moving subjects.
2. **Subject motion blur** – if your subject is moving and shutter speed is too slow, it'll blur even if the camera is steady.
3. **Missed focus** – autofocus locked onto the wrong point, or you're shooting with too wide an aperture (shallow depth of field) so only part of the scene is sharp.
4. **Wide aperture / shallow depth of field** – large apertures (small f-numbers) create a lot of background/foreground blur (bokeh) — great for portraits, bad if you wanted everything sharp.

**Quick fixes:**
- Increase shutter speed (raise ISO or open aperture if needed to compensate).
- Use a narrower aperture (higher f-number) for more in-focus area.
- Make sure autofocus is set to the right point/mode (single-shot for still subjects, continuous/tracking for moving ones).
- Stabilize the camera (support, tripod, or use in-body/lens image stabilization if available).
